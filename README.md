# Fallen Stocks — Public Screening Feed

This repository publishes a **read-only, public, end-of-day NSE/BSE
price-decline screen** for further research.

[Latest stock alerts (JSON)](stock-alerts.json)

The feed flags stocks whose unadjusted close declined more than 20% over
one of the most recent 1–7 trading-session windows. The principal research
window is 4–7 sessions; rapid 1–3-session falls are also included.

**These are NOT investment recommendations.** They are unverified price
moves that may reflect stock splits, bonuses, demergers, poor liquidity,
data errors, or permanent business problems. Corporate actions, the news
catalyst, sales, cash flows, debt, governance, product-market outlook and
valuation must be independently verified before judging any opportunity.

The feed contains only market-screening output. It has no broker,
account, server, portfolio or credential information.

Price data via [mtf.trading](https://mtf.trading/data), from NSE/BSE
disclosures. Generated by an independent, low-resource server batch job.
